# Telepico

Telepico is a minimal microblogging platform built around a simple idea: publishing should be easy, and feeds should be predictable.

Posts are published directly via a Telegram bot — no dashboards, no friction. The feed is strictly chronological — what people post is what you see.

Telepico also integrates with the fediverse via ActivityPub, meaning your posts can reach beyond the platform and interact with networks like Mastodon.

Telepico is an attempt to strip all of that away and go back to something simpler: a personal stream of thoughts, shared in real time — and not locked inside a single platform.
